Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

At SnapSweep ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information.

1. Core Philosophy: On-Device Processing

SnapSweep is built with privacy as a foundational principle.

  • No Cloud Uploads: All storage scanning, perceptual hashing, image quality analysis, and recommendations occur locally on your device.
  • Your photos, videos, screenshots, and personal metadata are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external remote servers controlled by us.

2. Information We Access

To provide core gallery and storage cleanup functionality, the App requests permission to access your local storage via Android MediaStore APIs:

  • Media Files (Images & Videos): Accessed locally to detect duplicates, similar photos, large files, and screenshots.
  • Storage Statistics: Read system storage space to calculate available capacity and provide breakdown categories.
This data is processed strictly in memory or cached locally in your app's private database for performance optimization and is never shared.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

To support a free version of the application, we may integrate standard advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These SDKs may collect standard non-personal diagnostic data or advertising IDs in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We do not share your personal media files with any advertisers.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You maintain absolute control over your files. SnapSweep operates under the principle: "AI recommends, user decides." No file is ever deleted automatically without your explicit manual review and confirmation. When you delete files, they are processed securely through Android system APIs (such as System Trash when supported).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@snapsweep.app.
